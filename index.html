Before You Join

Support group or group therapy?

We offer both, and they serve different purposes. Knowing the difference helps you choose the right fit.

Support Groups Support Group

  • A space to connect with others who understand what you're going through
  • Share experiences, encouragement, and accountability
  • Practice skills alongside peers, guided by a NECOA clinician

Group Therapy Therapy Group

  • Structured, clinician-led treatment with clear goals
  • Uses evidence-based approaches such as ERP, CBT, and ACT
  • Teaches and builds specific skills session by session

Good to know

Support groups are not therapy. They don't include an individual assessment or treatment plan, and they are not a substitute for individual treatment. Many people join one alongside their own therapy.

How Groups Fit In

How do groups add to individual treatment?

Groups reduce isolation, strengthen skills through shared learning and practice, and offer real-world chances to sit with anxiety in a supportive setting. Individual therapy provides personalized depth; group work adds connection, accountability, and confidence, so skills carry over more naturally into daily life.

Do I need to be a NECOA client?

No. Our groups are open to anyone who could benefit. People who are also in individual therapy at NECOA often find groups especially helpful, since they build on the same skills in both settings. Learn about our General Outpatient Services.

What does it cost?

Each group lists its own fee on the pages that follow. We are a self-pay clinic and do not accept insurance; see our FAQs for details.
